Waiting for the other player to join…

  • Take turns. On your turn, place a marble on any empty space, then rotate one of the four blocks a quarter turn in either direction.
  • The first player to get five marbles in a row, in any direction, wins.
  • Five in a row is checked after the rotation. If you already have five in a row after placing, you win without rotating.
  • If both players get five in a row from the same rotation, or the board fills up, it's a draw.
